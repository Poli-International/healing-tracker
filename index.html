Accelerate Healing with Antimicrobial Protection

Poli International Healing Discs are medical-grade elastomer discs designed to prevent irritation bumps, keloids, and infections during piercing healing. Using silver ion antimicrobial technology, these ultra-soft discs create a protective barrier between metal jewelry and healing skin, preventing bacterial growth while allowing proper oxygen flow.

🛡️ Prevents Irritation: Eliminates metal-to-skin contact during healing
🦠 Antimicrobial Protection: Silver ion technology prevents bacteria/virus growth
🎯 Ergonomic Design: Securely holds jewelry ball while protecting skin
✨ Medical Grade: Ultra-soft biocompatible elastomer for sensitive healing tissue

Especially recommended for cartilage piercings, navels, and any piercing prone to irritation bumps or keloid formation.

When to Seek Immediate Medical Attention

  • Fever over 101°F (38.3°C)
  • Severe pain that's getting worse
  • Red streaks spreading from the site
  • Difficulty breathing or chest tightness
  • Severe allergic reaction (hives, swelling of face/throat)
  • Signs of severe infection (green pus, extreme swelling)

If you experience any of these symptoms, contact your healthcare provider immediately or go to the emergency room.

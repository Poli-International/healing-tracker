# Tattoo & Piercing Healing Tracker, User Guide

## Overview

The Tattoo & Piercing Healing Tracker is a free tool from Poli International for studio clients, piercers and tattoo artists. It combines a stage-by-stage healing timeline, a daily healing log with photos, symptom trend analysis, aftercare checklists, product screeners and a tattoo touch-up planner in one page.

Everything runs in your browser. There is no account, and your entries and photos are saved in this browser's local storage on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. The tool is available in English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Portuguese.

**This tool does not diagnose.** It organises what you observe and shows typical healing patterns. If something worries you, contact your piercer, your tattoo artist or a doctor.

## Who it is for

- **Clients** following their own piercing or tattoo day by day.
- **Piercers and tattoo artists** who want to give clients a structured aftercare companion, or ask a client to bring a health report to a check-up.
- **Studio owners** who want to embed the tool on their own website (see the Embed tab).

## The Healing Tracker tab

### 1. Set up your healing journey

1. Choose **Piercing** or **Tattoo**.
2. Pick the placement. You can use the interactive **body map** (click or tab to a region, then press Enter) or the lists: 54 piercing placements, or 16 tattoo placements plus a tattoo size (small, medium or large).
3. Enter the **procedure date** and, if you like, a custom name such as "Left conch".
4. Optionally add a **Day 0 baseline photo**. It becomes the reference for later comparisons.
5. Click **Start Tracking My Healing**.

When you select a placement that gets a lot of sun (face, ears, neck, hands, shoulders and similar), a **sun exposure advisory** explains how to protect the area while it heals.

### 2. Your healing timeline

Shows the typical healing time for your placement, the day of healing you are on, and each healing stage with what to expect and how to care for it. **The stage you are in now is highlighted**, and the same stage appears as the current phase in the side panel. Cartilage and other placements prone to irritation bumps include a healing disc recommendation.

### 3. Check your current symptoms

Tick any of the 15 listed symptoms and click **Assess My Symptoms**. The result is grouped as normal healing, monitor closely, or concerning. Anything in the concerning group (for example red streaks, fever over 38.3°C / 101°F, green or foul discharge) means you should contact a professional promptly.

### 4. Today's aftercare checklist

A daily checklist matched to piercings or tattoos. **Reset Today's Checklist** clears the ticks for the next day. Below it:

- **Studio Pro-Tips** change with your healing stage (for example when to plan downsizing, or why to avoid swimming).
- **Daily hydration check-in** counts glasses of water, with an optional reminder interval.

### 5. Daily healing log

Click **Log Today's Entry** and rate redness, swelling, tenderness and discharge from 0 to 3. For piercings you can also rate how tight the jewelry feels. Add a photo and notes if you want. Photos are compressed and their location data is removed before saving.

If your ratings are higher than last time, the log asks **what changed** (knocked it, slept on it, new jewelry, new product, swimming, gym, another piercing nearby, other). It records the likely trigger; it does not diagnose.

A storage meter shows how much browser space is left. When space runs low you can drop the oldest photos while keeping every rating and note.

### 6. Milestone gallery and photo comparison

- **Gallery grid:** every photo in date order, with its day number, ratings and notes. Click a photo to open it full size and step through the others.
- **Split comparison slider:** choose two photos and drag the divider to compare them.

### 7. Symptom severity comparison

Choose any two log entries. A bar chart shows each rating side by side, each symptom is marked improved, stable or elevated, and the total score (out of 12) is compared.

### 8. Healing and symptom calendar

A month view of your log. Each logged day shows a coloured dot for its highest rating and its healing day number. The header counts days logged, your current run of consecutive days, and your peak discomfort day. Click a day to see its entries.

### 9. Symptom trends over time

Once you have at least two entries, a chart plots redness, swelling, tenderness and discharge over time, with a short sentence describing each trend.

### 10. Alerts

- **Symptom trend alert:** shown when redness or swelling rises across three consecutive entries. It appears above the results, in the side panel and in the health report.
- **Trend reversal alert:** shown when symptoms had been improving and then rise again over several entries.

Both alerts recommend an in-person check with your piercer or tattoo artist. They are prompts to get looked at, not a diagnosis.

### 11. Piercing tension gauge (piercings only)

Uses the tightness ratings from your log. It shows the current level (0 normal, 1 snug, 2 moderately tight, 3 constricting) and counts consecutive tight days. If tightness lasts more than 7 days it advises a studio visit to check the post length or plan downsizing.

### 12. Jewelry longevity and downsizing calculator (piercings only)

Starts from your tracked placement and procedure date. Choose the jewelry material and closure type to see:

- the typical **downsizing window** for the placement, with target dates for a studio visit;
- the expected **lifespan of the material and its finish**;
- how often to **check the closure** (threadless, internally threaded, hinged segment or captive bead ring), with maintenance tips.

BioFlex® body jewelry (medical-grade PP-R) is treated as suitable for long-term wear: inspect it at every check and replace it if it is scratched, bent or discoloured, as you would any jewelry.

### 13. Healing health report

**Generate Health Report** builds a summary of your procedure, the current and peak ratings, the trajectory, any trend alert, and every logged entry. You can print it or save it as PDF, copy it as text, or download a text file to share with your studio or doctor.

### 14. Healing journey snapshot

**Generate Infographic** draws a single image with your procedure, days elapsed, progress, number of entries and your baseline and latest photos. Download it as PNG or copy it to send to your artist or piercer.

### 15. Product allergy and sensitivity screener

Choose the product category (ointment or balm, piercing cleanser, barrier film, daily lotion, antiseptic soap), read the guidance for that category, and answer six questions about your history (reactions to antibiotic ointments, adhesives, fragrances, lanolin or latex, harsh ingredients, eczema near the site). The result is a low, moderate or high sensitivity risk with the reasons, and a patch test method for moderate and high results.

### 16. Product ingredient scanner

Paste an ingredient list or try a sample preset. The scanner checks each ingredient against its list of known contact allergens, drying alcohols, occlusives and cytotoxic ingredients and shows what it matched, why it matters and a suggested alternative. **A clean result only means nothing on its list was found. It is not proof that a product is safe.**

### 17. Backup and restore

Export all your pieces, entries and photos to a JSON file, for example to move from phone to computer. When restoring you can **replace** everything or **merge** the backup with what is already there.

## The Touch-Up Timeline tab (tattoos)

- **Touch-up calculator:** enter the tattoo's age, last touch-up (or "never touched up"), style, placement, ink colours, sun exposure and aftercare level. You get the projected time until a touch-up is advisable, the share of expected longevity already used, and the likely order in which the colours fade. You can save a **touch-up reminder**; it is stored in this browser, and when a touch-up is due soon or overdue a banner reminds you on your next visit.
- **Fading predictor:** how the tattoo is likely to change year by year, and how different care routines change that.
- **Maintenance plan:** a care schedule for the long term.
- **Lifetime costs:** a 30-year estimate of touch-up sessions and spending with good care versus poor care.
- **Touch-up readiness:** a short assessment of whether the skin and tattoo are ready for a touch-up session now.

All projections are estimates based on typical fading patterns. Your artist's assessment of the actual tattoo comes first.

## The Documentation and Embed tabs

- **Documentation** shows the technical documentation in the language you select.
- **Embed** gives you an iframe code to place the tool on your own website. It loads from poliinternational.com and keeps each visitor's data in their own browser.

## Privacy

Entries, photos, reminders and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data deletes them, so use **Backup and restore** if you want to keep a copy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I track more than one piercing or tattoo?
Yes. Each procedure you start keeps its own log and photos. To return to one, set it up again with the same type, placement and date.

### Is my data sent anywhere?
No. Everything is calculated and stored in your browser.

### The tool shows a concerning result. What should I do?
Contact your piercer, tattoo artist or a doctor promptly. With a piercing, do not remove the jewelry on your own if an infection is suspected: removing it can close the channel and trap the infection. Ask a professional first.

### Why is a stage highlighted in my timeline?
It is the stage your procedure date places you in. Healing speed varies from person to person, so use it as a guide, not a deadline.

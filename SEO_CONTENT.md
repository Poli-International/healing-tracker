## What is Piercing Healing Tracker?

The **Piercing Healing Tracker** is a free web tool that provides personalized healing timelines and aftercare guidance for over 40 piercing types. Users select their procedure type (piercing or tattoo), choose a specific body location from an extensive dropdown menu, and enter the date of their procedure. The tool then generates a structured healing timeline with stage-by-stage expectations, symptom checklists, and daily aftercare tasks.

The tool includes a symptom checker that categorizes current symptoms as normal, monitor closely, or concerning, with appropriate guidance for each level. It also provides a daily aftercare checklist that resets each day, helping clients maintain consistent care routines throughout their healing journey.

## Who Should Use This Tool

**Piercing studio owners and piercers:** Use this tool to provide clients with standardized, location-specific healing timelines. Embed it on your studio website to reduce aftercare questions and improve client outcomes.

**New piercing clients:** Track your healing progress day by day. Know what symptoms are normal versus when to seek help. Get clear aftercare instructions specific to your piercing type.

**Aftercare educators:** Use the structured timeline format to teach clients about healing phases, expected symptoms, and proper care techniques.

**Apprentice piercers:** Study the healing timelines for different piercing locations. Understand how healing varies by anatomy and location.

## How to Use

### Step 1: Set Up Your Healing Journey

1. **Select Procedure Type:** Choose "Piercing" from the dropdown menu. (The tool also supports "Tattoo" for tattoo healing tracking.)

2. **Choose Piercing Location:** Select your specific piercing from the categorized dropdown. Options include:
   - Ear piercings: earlobe, helix, industrial, daith, rook, tragus, anti-tragus, conch, snug, forward helix, orbital
   - Facial piercings: nostril, septum, bridge, high nostril, eyebrow, anti-eyebrow, cheek, medusa, monroe, labret, vertical labret, snake bites, angel bites, smiley, frowny
   - Oral piercings: tongue, tongue web, venom, uvula
   - Body piercings: navel, nipple
   - Surface and dermal piercings: surface, dermal anchor, nape, sternum, madison
   - Male genital piercings: prince albert, reverse PA, ampallang, apadravya, frenum, lorum, hafada, dydoe, guiche, foreskin
   - Female genital piercings: VCH, HCH, inner labia, outer labia, triangle, christina, fourchette

3. **Enter Procedure Date:** Select the date your piercing was performed.

4. **Click "Start Tracking My Healing":** The tool generates your personalized timeline.

### Step 2: View Your Healing Timeline

The timeline shows:
- Your piercing name and typical healing time
- Days elapsed since your procedure
- Stage-by-stage breakdown with:
  - Expected symptoms for each stage
  - Specific care instructions for each stage

### Step 3: Check Your Current Symptoms

1. Select any symptoms you're currently experiencing from the grid of 15 options.
2. Click "Assess My Symptoms."
3. The tool categorizes your symptoms as:
   - **Normal:** Typical healing symptoms (mild redness, slight swelling, clear discharge, mild tenderness, itching)
   - **Monitor Closely:** Symptoms that need attention (moderate swelling, increased redness, yellow/white discharge, warmth)
   - **Concerning:** Symptoms requiring medical attention (severe pain, fever over 101°F, red streaks spreading, green/foul discharge, excessive swelling, persistent bleeding)

### Step 4: Complete Today's Aftercare Checklist

The checklist provides daily tasks specific to your procedure type:
- For piercings: clean with saline, wash hands, avoid rotating jewelry, check for infection signs
- For tattoos: wash gently, apply lotion, avoid picking, protect from sun

Click "Reset Today's Checklist" to uncheck all items for the next day.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: How do I start tracking my piercing healing?

Select "Piercing" from the procedure type dropdown, choose your specific piercing location from the categorized list, enter the date you got pierced, and click "Start Tracking My Healing."

### Q2: What piercing locations are supported?

The tool supports over 40 piercing locations across ear, facial, oral, body, surface/dermal, male genital, and female genital categories. See the full list in the "How to Use" section above.

### Q3: Can I track tattoo healing with this tool?

Yes. Select "Tattoo" as the procedure type, then choose your tattoo size (small under 2 inches, medium 2-6 inches, or large over 6 inches). The tool generates a tattoo-specific healing timeline.

### Q4: How does the symptom checker work?

Select any symptoms you're currently experiencing from the 15-option grid. The tool categorizes them as normal, monitor closely, or concerning, and provides appropriate guidance for each level.

### Q5: What symptoms require medical attention?

Severe pain, fever over 101°F, red streaks spreading from the site, green or foul-smelling discharge, excessive swelling, and persistent bleeding all require immediate medical attention.

### Q6: How often should I use the symptom checker?

Use it whenever you notice new symptoms or changes in your piercing. The tool helps you distinguish between normal healing and potential problems.

### Q7: Can I reset the aftercare checklist daily?

Yes. Click "Reset Today's Checklist" to uncheck all items. The checklist is designed for daily use throughout your healing journey.

### Q8: Does the tool work for all healing stages?

Yes. The timeline covers all healing stages from initial healing through complete maturation, with stage-specific symptoms and care instructions for each phase.

### Q9: How accurate are the healing timelines?

Healing times are based on industry standards and vary by location. The tool provides typical ranges (e.g., "6-8 weeks" for earlobes, "6-12 months" for industrial piercings). Individual healing times may vary.

### Q10: Can I use this tool for multiple piercings?

The tool tracks one piercing at a time. For multiple piercings, complete the setup process separately for each one.

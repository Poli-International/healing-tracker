## 🎯 Overview

The Tattoo & Piercing Healing Timeline & Aftercare Tracker is a professional, client-side digital companion for monitoring recovery across tattoos and 54 body piercing placements. Users track recovery day-by-day, log photo entries, compare healing states with an interactive split slider, assess product sensitivities, and project tattoo longevity with proactive touch-up calculations.

**Key Use Cases:**

- Tracking daily healing phases and anatomical recovery milestones
- Logging aftercare routines and daily hygiene checklists
- Comparing healing photos with an interactive split slider and severity charts
- Generating printable, studio-ready clinical recovery summaries
- Screening aftercare lotions, cleansers, and barrier films for contact allergens
- Estimating long-term tattoo fading and maintenance savings over 30 years
- Triage of symptoms distinguishing normal healing from critical infection signs

**Target Audience:**

- Piercing and tattoo studio clients
- Professional body piercers and tattoo artists (client aftercare guidance)
- Studio owners seeking free, embeddable aftercare tools

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Healing timelines with the current stage marked**: 54 piercing placements and tattoos by size, each stage with what to expect and how to care for it.
- ✅ **Body map and sun advisory**: pick the placement on an interactive map; high-UV placements get sun protection guidance.
- ✅ **Symptom check and daily aftercare checklist**, with stage-specific studio tips and a hydration check-in.
- ✅ **Daily healing log**: redness, swelling, tenderness, discharge and jewelry tightness on 0-3, photos (location data removed), notes, and a "what changed" prompt when symptoms rise.
- ✅ **Milestone gallery, photo viewer and split-slider comparison.**
- ✅ **Calendar, trend chart and side-by-side severity comparison.**
- ✅ **Worsening-trend and trend-reversal alerts** that recommend an in-person check.
- ✅ **Piercing tension gauge and jewelry longevity calculator**: downsizing window, material lifespan, closure check schedule.
- ✅ **Allergy screener and ingredient scanner** for aftercare products (a clean scan is not proof of safety).
- ✅ **Health report and journey snapshot** to print, copy or share with a studio.
- ✅ **Touch-Up Timeline tab**: touch-up estimate and reminder, fading predictor, maintenance plan, 30-year costs, readiness check.
- ✅ **Backup and restore** (replace or merge).
- ✅ **Private by design**: runs in the browser; data stays in localStorage on the device.
- ✅ **Seven languages**: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Dutch and Portuguese.
- ✅ **Free and open source**: MIT licence.
